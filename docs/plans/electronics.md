## Current Understandings

I've come to understand pcb design skill in terms of usage of integrated circuits. Signs of skills:
- Being able to implement functionality with a IC
- Having well developed engineering intuition(quick complex maths) about usage and implementation of a part.

Other less understood areas of electronic design:
- PCB form factors (flex, semi flex, different board materials)
- RF Design: PCB Antenna Design and Selection
- Power Electronic Design
- AC & DC design

### Future Course Work
